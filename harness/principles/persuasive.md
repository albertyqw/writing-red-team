# Persuasive

For essays, pitches, and applications that ask a reader to act or believe.

## Whole piece
- The claim or ask comes first, then the case
- The strongest objection is answered, not just named
- Evidence scales with how surprising the claim is
- The reader knows what to do or believe by the end

## Prose
- Concrete examples over abstractions
- One idea per paragraph, led by its point
