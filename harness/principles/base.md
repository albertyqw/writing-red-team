# Base

Always applied.

## Whole piece
- Every section earns its place against the goal in the brief
- One main claim, stated where the reader will see it

## Prose
- Orwell's six rules: no stale figures of speech; short words over long; cut every word you can; active over passive; plain English over jargon and foreign phrases; break any rule sooner than write something barbarous
- No filler, no marketing language
- Simple punctuation that keeps the reader moving
