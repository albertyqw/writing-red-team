# Scientific

For research proposals, papers, and technical write-ups.

## Whole piece
- Question, hypothesis, method, and predicted result are each explicit
- Every construct maps to a measure
- Claims match the strength of the evidence; limits are stated
- Prior work is described fairly, and the contribution beyond it is clear

## Prose
- Each term defined once and used one way
- Numbers agree across sections
- Hedges only where uncertainty is real
