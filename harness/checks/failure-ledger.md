# Failure ledger

Recurring problems, seeded from three drafts in November 2026. A line here can be promoted to its own check file in this folder.

1. The concrete claim or ask arrives late, after framing
2. A key term is used before it is defined, or defined more than one way
3. A large empirical or quantitative claim has no source or derivation
4. A list of options stands where a commitment is needed
5. Scope widens near the end
6. The strongest objection is named as a risk and left unanswered
7. Insider references appear without a gloss
8. Hedges stack
9. Runs of rhetorical questions stand in for claims
10. Voice shifts between "we" and "I"
11. Borrowed business idiom the style rules would cut
12. Research: no stated prediction per arm; constructs and measures not mapped; numbers disagree between sections
